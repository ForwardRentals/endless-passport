Truncated Travels: Magical Morocco
Fall in love with the vibrant colors, stunning sites, and electrifying energy of Morocco. World traveler Brian
Michalski shares two months here. We will highlight two exceptional destinations: beachy, alternative Taghazout,
and the blue gem of Chefchaouen. In addition to learning about culture, history, and economy, we will get whisked
away by ATV and camel into the desert, along with climbing majestic Mount Toubkal. Learn about Harira soup,
chicken tagine, vegetable couscous, and a Q&A for dessert.
Truncated Travels: Picturesque Portugal
Thanks to its jaw-dropping beauty and hospitable locals, five weeks in Portugal made globetrotter Brian Michalski
feel born again. Come along as we marvel at the country’s Crown Jewels, the Azores Islands, and explore
picture-perfect Lagos by land and sea. The fabric of Portugal is rich: examine its economy, way of life, plants,
animals, and proud traditions. Get a hearty education about bacalhau fritters, caldo verde soup, and sweet pastéis
de nata. We conclude with a decadent Q&A.
